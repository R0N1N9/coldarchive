Universe

A whistleblower hid proof of an illegal autonomous-drone program inside the Halden compute campus before they vanished. The evidence sits on a cold archive server, air-gapped behind guards, cameras and patrol drones. One of you goes in on foot. The other rides the network. The agent can't open a door without overwatch; overwatch can't touch the deep network until the agent plants the relays. Every map is generated fresh.

Operation Cold Archive

The job

  1. Slip through the cut in the fence and pick the maintenance door.
  2. Plant a relay at the utility junction — overwatch gets the building network.
  3. Lift the keycard off the Site Chief. Overwatch can find him on the cameras.
  4. Pick into the network room and plant the second relay — overwatch gets the core.
  5. Swipe into the archive vault while overwatch cracks the lock.
  6. Hold the uplink while the evidence copies to the cold drive. Overwatch fights SENTINEL's sweep.
  7. Pull the drive. SENTINEL seals the building.
  8. Overwatch reopens the dock. Carry the drive to the van.
